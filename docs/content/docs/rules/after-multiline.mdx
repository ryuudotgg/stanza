title: after-multiline
description: The blank line after a statement that spans lines.
---

<RuleSummary />

<DiffExample fixture="after-multiline/example.ts" />

A statement is multi-line when it starts and ends on different lines: a call with its arguments on their own lines, an object literal, an `if` whose block body spans lines, a loop, a `try`. The rule applies to every gap after such a statement, at every level below module top level.

Two rules outrank it. [short-body](/rules/short-body) removes the blank when the list holds two or three compact statements, and [guard-chain](/rules/guard-chain) removes it between two braceless two line guards. It outranks every join rule, so a multi-line declaration never joins the `if`, `return`, loop or `try` that reads it. The example shows that: the guard reads `raw`, and the blank line still goes in.

When the next statement begins on the line where the multi-line statement ends, nothing is inserted between them. A switch clause whose body starts on the line after its label is multi-line too, so the blank line before the next label is reported under this rule rather than [switch-clauses](/rules/switch-clauses).
