---
title: after-guard
description: The blank line after a guard that returns, throws, continues or breaks.
---

<RuleSummary />

A guard here is an `if` without `else` whose single body statement is `return`, `throw`, `continue` or `break`. When the statement after it is anything but another `if` or a jump, a blank line goes between them: the guard closes one step and the next statement opens another. In the example each `throw` guard gets a blank line after it, while `const slug` stays tight against the guard that reads it through [guard-join](/rules/guard-join).

Every rule earlier in the order outranks it: [short-body](/rules/short-body), [guard-chain](/rules/guard-chain), [after-multiline](/rules/after-multiline), [switch-clauses](/rules/switch-clauses) and the join rules. A guard that spans two lines gets the same blank line, reported as [after-multiline](/rules/after-multiline).

An `if` whose block holds two statements is not a guard, so nothing is inserted after `if (!item) { log("none"); return; }`.
