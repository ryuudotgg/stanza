### 🐛 Line width overrides match files like Bun 1.4.4

The `overrides` globs of oxfmt, Prettier and Biome configs now pick the files Bun 1.4.4's `Bun.Glob` picks when Stanza works out a file's line width. A pattern with many brace alternatives, or braces nested more than ten deep, used to give up partway and miss a file it names, and could take over 100 ms per file. It now decides as Bun does in well under a millisecond. Extglobs such as `@(a|b)` and ranges such as `{1..3}` match too. A few patterns change the other way: `{a,b` with no closing brace no longer matches `a`, while an unclosed `{a` matches a file named `{a`, a pattern ending in `\` matches that backslash, and `{**}` matches paths inside folders.
